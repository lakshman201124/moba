# MOBA - Real Menu Structure

> Source of truth: `assets/moba-branding/Moba menu dump Aug 2026.pdf` (14 pages: intro/brand story, 12 category pages split across 13 sheets, desserts). This file maps that print menu into the categories, option patterns and data model the digital menu needs. It replaces the placeholder items (Teri Gochujang, ₹329...) seen in the early mockups: those were illustrative only.

All prices are in INR and exclude taxes (the PDF footer says "ALL PRICES IN INR - TAXES EXTRA").

---

## 1. Brand story to use in the experience (from PDF page 1)

- **MOBA (noun, Korean)** = **모으다 (moeuda)**: "to gather, to bring together" **+ 밥 (bap)**: "rice, and in Korea, any shared meal".
- **GATHER FOR FOOD.** "In Korea you don't ask a friend how they are. You ask 밥 먹었어? - have you eaten? That's the whole idea behind our name."
- **MADE HERE. EVERY DAY. (직접 만듭니다)**: every sauce built in the kitchen from whole ingredients (gochujang blends, bulgogi glaze, buldak); noodles, pastes and toppings made in house; no artificial flavours or colours; nothing from a packet.
- Trust chips: **100% IN-HOUSE SAUCES / NO ARTIFICIAL FLAVOURS / NO ARTIFICIAL COLOURS / FRESH FRUIT, NEVER SYRUP**.
- Sign-off: **같이 밥 먹어요 - LET'S EAT TOGETHER**. Tagline line: "Est. in Seoul flavours - made in our kitchen". Footer sign-off on every page: 맛있게 드세요! (enjoy your meal).

This is the creative engine for the "people gather for food" opening (see `visual-direction.md`, section 0F).

## 3. Category detail

### 3.1 Moba Signatures `모바 시그니처` - "Our two most-loved plates"
- **RABOKKI** `SIGNATURE` (ramyeon + tteokbokki in one pan, every bowl 369, choice of Tofu / Paneer / Chicken / Fish): Classic Rabokki `HOT`, Creamy Rabokki `MILD`, Bulgogi Rabokki `MILD`, Flamey Buldak Rabokki `VERY HOT` (all 369).
- Explainer panel: RAMEYON 라면 + TOPPOKI 떡볶이 = RABOKKI 라보끼: "Korea's original ban-ban, half and half".
- **KOREAN CREAM CHEESE BUN** `SIGNATURE` 279, `CONTAINS EGG`; six pull-apart petals, long cheese pull. Note: baked with egg, not suitable for egg-avoiding vegetarians.
- Add-ons: Fried Egg 25, Extra Rameyon Egg 25, Kimchi on the Side 40, Extra Meat/Veg 50.

### 3.2 Korean Starters - "Tossed to order, Seoul street-food style"
Fiery Seoul Gochujang `HOT` 289 (base: cauliflower / chicken / meat) - Smoky Seoul Gochujang `HOT` 289 (same bases) - Flamey Buldak `VERY HOT` 289 (mushroom / chicken / meat) - Seoul Bulgogi Toss `MILD` 289 (potato / chicken / meat) - Gangnam Honey Chilli Garlic `MILD` 289 (chicken / potato) - Butter Garlic Prawns `MILD` 339 - Crispy Tempura Prawns `MILD` 339.
- **Choose your base** panel: cauliflower, potato, mushroom (keep it veg), chicken, meat (non-veg); prawns as served.
- **BAN-BAN half & half**: large only, +149, mix two flavours in one plate, can pair a veg base with a non-veg one.

### 3.3 Korean Crispy Bites - "Fried fresh, sauced to order"
- Korean Fried Chicken: Wings (small 4 pcs 229 / large 6 pcs 289) and Strips (small 4 pcs 229 / large 6 pcs 289).
- Korean Fried Tofu "Crispy Tofu" small 229 / large 289. Korean Fried Cauliflower "Crispy Florets" small 229 / large 289.
- Every one: **pick any flavour**. Flavour options (8): Gochuchang, Topokki, Bulgogi, Buldak `EXTRA HOT`, Smoky Gochu, Sweet Chilli, Peri Peri Dusting, Ban-Ban (any two flavours, large only).
- **MAKE A MEAL +99** (add any one): Crispy Fries (salted / peri peri), Classic Lemonade, Classic Cold Coffee, Lemon Ice Tea.

### 3.4 Street Snacks `길거리 간식` - corn doggs, nachos, fries
- **K-Corn Dogg** 콘도그 (every dogg 289): Mozzarella Cheese, Ramyeon (veg or chicken), Chicken, Chicken & Cheese (50:50).
- **K-Nachos** 나쵸 (every platter 289, choice of Tofu / Paneer / Chicken): Fiery Korean Nachos, Seoul Street Nachos (MOBA signature), Bulgogi Nachos.
- **K-Street Bites** 스트리트 간식: Crispy Fries 189 (salted / peri peri), Korean Fries 219, Korean Loaded Fries 259 (chicken / tofu), K-Spring Rolls 189, K-Pop Cheese Balls 189.

### 3.5 Korean Mandu - "Steamed to order, then served your way"
1. **Choose your mandu** (hand-folded): Classic Veg 289, Cheezy Spicy Veg 289, Spicy Chicken 289, Cheese & Chicken 289, Prawn Hargao 369.
2. **Choose how it's served**: **Saucy Plate** (over velvety gochujang sauce with sauteed vegetables) or **Broth Bowl** (warming Korean broth with gochujang and vegetables).

### 3.6 Salads & Soups - "Fresh, light, comforting"
- **Korean Salads** (geotjeori style, dressed to order; choice of chicken / fish / paneer / tofu): Sangchu Geotjeori 289, Korean Japchae 299, Bok Choy Geotjeori 289.
- **Korean Soups** (same protein choice): Lemongrass Soup 189, Creamy Broccoli Soup 199, Spicy Gochu Coconut Guk 199.
- Good to know: salads travel best eaten fresh; soups come in a shareable bowl, ask for an extra bowl. Paneer and tofu keep a dish vegetarian.

### 3.7 Gimbap & Toppoki `김밥 / 떡볶이` - "Rolled & simmered to order"

### 3.8 Rameyon `라면` - "Slurp-worthy bowls, every bowl 339"

### 3.9 K-Rice & Noodle - "One menu, your way: build it in four steps"

## 5. Content issues to resolve with the client

1. **Spelling is inconsistent**: "Gochuchang" (Rameyon, Crispy Bites) vs "Gochujang" (Starters, Gimbap); "Toppoki" vs the mockups' "Tteokbokki"; "Rameyon" vs "Ramyeon" (used in the sentence copy and the corn dogg name). Pick one canonical spelling per word before the data is entered.
2. **Some items and prices in the early mockups are not on the real menu**: Teri Gochujang, Spicy Soy Ramen, rameyon prices of 299-339, a 4-level spice guide (real: 3 levels), fillings Chicken / Beef / Tofu / Seafood (real: Tofu / Paneer / Chicken / Fish, no beef), and the hero dishes Jjajangmyeon and Bibimbap. Do not reuse them. Real Rameyon is 7 flavours at a flat 339; there is no Jjajangmyeon, Bibimbap or Korean Fried Chicken *bowl* card on the menu (fried chicken exists as Wings/Strips in Crispy Bites).
3. **Dish photography:** the PDF has photos or illustrated bowls for about half the categories (Tofu and Cauliflower use a cartoon blue bowl as a placeholder). We need a per-category hero shot list (see `implementation.md`).
4. **Page 14** of the PDF is the Desserts page; no address, hours, phone or social handles appear anywhere in the PDF. Ask for them if a footer / info section is wanted.
5. Missing `EGG` marks and double marks need a rule in the data (diet is decided by the chosen base).
