## Scope & mode
The MOBA digital-menu home experience: one long-scroll, mobile-first page scanned from a QR code at the table. Mode: Persuade -> Operate (the gather/TV opening sells the vibe; the menu section operates). This brief covers the whole home page; the colored design pass and DESIGN.md follow wireframe approval.

## Audience / job / action / constraints
In-restaurant diners on their phones, hungry, choosing food. Job: feel the brand, then browse all 12 categories and read any dish. No ordering, no cart, no accounts - static, browse-only. Fast after a QR scan on 4G.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A Korean street-food TV channel you scroll through. The hero is a vintage TV playing a "gather for food" ad; a 2D anime ramen bowl detaches and travels down the scroll, threading the sections together. It refuses the standard restaurant-site hero-photo-plus-nav; the screen is a broadcast, not a brochure.

OWN-WORLD: MOBA street-pop. 60% orange `#FF531B`, 30% pale yellow `#F6F396`, 10% light blue `#8BBEE9`, cobalt `#3069B8` ink-outline, ink `#1B1A2E`, cream `#FFF8E7`. Bagel Fat One display (uppercase, Hangul), rounded-grotesk body. Thick cobalt outlines, sticker badges, scalloped awning edges, giant cropped Hangul behind content, grey/B&W line illustrations (pojangmacha stall), the deadpan bear, speech bubbles, sparkles. (Wireframe stage renders this world in greyscale only.)

STORY: The visitor scans, lands in a loud, funny, unmistakably Korean space, understands "MOBA = gather for food, made in-house," is pulled down by the traveling bowl, and browses all 12 menu categories, tapping any dish for a bottom sheet.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Top - slim bar with the MOBA logo and a MENU control. Below - giant Bagel Fat One headline "GATHER FOR FOOD" with a one-line subhead and a "VIEW MENU" pill. Dominating the lower hero - a large retro TV (the star) playing the vintage gather/ad clip, with a rotating seal badge ("EST. IN SEOUL FLAVOURS") pinned to it, and the 2D anime ramen bowl resting beside it ready to travel. A grey pojangmacha street-stall line illustration grounds the base on a scalloped awning edge. Primary action (VIEW MENU) sits under the headline and repeats as a sticky pill.

FORM: Pinned reference-composition (Future Noodles split hero with the framed subject replaced by the TV + traveling bowl; Chompo's long-scroll section system below), set in the established MOBA world. User-pinned, no concept roll. Seed key: n/a (pinned direction, roll skipped per new-work "a user- or brief-pinned direction beats the roll").

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
